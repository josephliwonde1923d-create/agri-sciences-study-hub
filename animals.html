🐄 About Animal Production

Animal production involves the responsible management of livestock to produce food, income and other useful products while maintaining good animal welfare.

🌾 Good Livestock Practices

Proper Feeding

Provide animals with adequate and balanced feed together with clean water.

Animal Health

Regular health monitoring, vaccination and good hygiene help prevent livestock diseases.

Good Housing

Suitable housing protects animals from harsh weather and helps maintain good health and productivity.
