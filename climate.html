🌱 What is Climate-Smart Agriculture?

Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) is an approach that helps farmers increase agricultural productivity, adapt to climate change and reduce greenhouse gas emissions where possible.

🌾 CSA Practices

Conservation Agriculture

Practices such as minimum soil disturbance, soil cover and crop rotation help improve soil health.

Crop Diversification

Growing different crops can improve resilience, support soil health and reduce production risks.

Water Management

Efficient water management helps farmers cope with dry periods and irregular rainfall.

Agroforestry

Integrating trees with crops or livestock can improve soil protection, biodiversity and farm resilience.

☀️ Benefits of CSA

Higher Resilience

Helps farming communities better cope with droughts, floods and changing weather patterns.

Improved Soil Health

Sustainable practices can help protect soil from erosion and maintain its productivity.

Sustainable Production

CSA promotes agricultural production while paying attention to environmental sustainability.
